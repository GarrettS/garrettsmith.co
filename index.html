How I Work

Training built on understanding

Mechanism Teaching

Every exercise has a reason. I explain how your muscles, joints, and nervous system interact so you understand why something works — not just what to do. Clients who understand their body make better decisions outside the gym.

Movement Assessment

Before programming a single rep, I assess how you move. A structured screen reveals compensation patterns, restrictions, and asymmetries that generic programs ignore. What we find shapes everything that follows.

Custom Programming

Your program adapts to your movement patterns, recovery capacity, and goals — not the other way around. No templates, no one-size-fits-all progressions. Programming evolves as you do.

Movement Quality

Good movement is the foundation. I prioritize how well you move over how much you lift. The result is durable strength that transfers to life outside the gym — whether that's playing with your kids, competing in a sport, or just feeling capable.
